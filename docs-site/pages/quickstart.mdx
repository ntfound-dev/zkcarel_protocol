# Quick Start

CAREL Protocol is live on Starknet Sepolia testnet. You need a Starknet wallet to get started.

## Step 1 — Get a Starknet Wallet

Install [Argent X](https://www.argent.xyz/argent-x/) or [Braavos](https://braavos.app/) browser extension. Create a new wallet and switch to **Starknet Sepolia** testnet.

## Step 2 — Get Testnet Tokens

You need testnet tokens to pay for transactions.

- **STRK testnet**: use the [Starknet Sepolia Faucet](https://starknet-faucet.vercel.app/)
- **CAREL testnet tokens**: available from the faucet inside the CAREL app (Rewards → Faucet)

## Step 3 — Connect Your Wallet

Go to [carel-protocol.vercel.app](https://carel-protocol.vercel.app) and click **Connect Wallet**. Select your Starknet wallet. Once connected, your balance will appear in the top bar.

## Step 4 — Make Your First Swap

1. Open the **Trade** tab
2. Select tokens to swap (e.g. STRK → USDT)
3. Enter amount
4. Click **Get Quote**
5. Review the preview and click **Confirm Swap**
6. Approve the transaction in your wallet

Your swap will be routed through the Swap Aggregator contract and confirmed on-chain.

## Step 5 — Try Hide Mode

Hide Mode routes your transaction through the CAREL relayer so your wallet address is not linked to the on-chain transaction.

1. On the Trade tab, toggle **Hide Mode** on
2. Select a supported flow: Swap, Stake, or Limit Order
3. Enter your details and click **Get Quote**
4. Sign the intent payload in your wallet (this is not a transaction — it is a signature)
5. The backend generates a Garaga Honk proof and the relayer submits on-chain

The on-chain transaction will show the relayer's wallet, not yours.

> **Note:** Hide Mode requires the backend prover to be available. If proving is unavailable, the UI will indicate this. Hide Mode is testnet-only at this stage.

## Step 6 — Try the AI Agent

1. Open the **AI** panel (floating button, bottom right)
2. Type a command: `"swap 10 STRK to USDT"` or `"stake 100 CAREL"`
3. The AI agent will parse your intent and show an execution plan
4. Confirm to execute — the agent handles routing and on-chain submission

For L3 (private AI execution), select AI Level 3 in settings. This combines AI routing with Hide Mode.

## Earn Points

Every on-chain action earns points:

| Action | Base rate |
|---|---|
| Swap | 10 pts/USD |
| Limit Order | 12 pts/USD |
| ETH Bridge | 15 pts/USD |
| BTC Bridge | 25 pts/USD |
| Stake | 3 pts/USD |

Points multiply with staking tier (up to 5x for CAREL stakers) and get a bonus in Hide Mode.

Check your points and rank on the **Leaderboard** tab.
