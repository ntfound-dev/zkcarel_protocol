# Staking

CAREL Protocol has four staking contracts — each accepts a different asset and earns points at different rates. Staking increases your points multiplier, which increases your share of the monthly airdrop.

## Points Multiplier by Staked CAREL

Staking CAREL unlocks a global multiplier applied to all your points:

| Staked CAREL | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| < 100 | 1× (no multiplier) |
| ≥ 100 | 2× |
| ≥ 1,000 | 3× |
| ≥ 10,000 | 5× |

The multiplier applies to all actions — swap, bridge, stake, referral — not just the staking action itself.

## Points per Staking Action

Staking earns base points on top of the multiplier:

| Asset | Points |
|---|---|
| CAREL | 3 pts/USD staked |
| Stablecoin (USDT/USDC/STRK) | 3 pts/USD staked × 1× |
| WBTC | 3 pts/USD staked × 1.5× |
| LP tokens | 3 pts/USD staked × 5× |

## How to Stake

1. Go to the **Stake** tab in the CAREL app
2. Select the asset you want to stake
3. Enter the amount
4. Click **Stake** and approve the transaction in your wallet
5. Your staked balance appears immediately — points start accruing in the current epoch

## Unstaking

You can unstake at any time. There is no lock-up period on testnet. Unstaking reduces your multiplier if your remaining balance drops below a tier threshold.

## Why Stake

Staking serves two purposes:

1. **Points multiplier** — higher stake = higher multiplier on all your actions = larger share of the monthly airdrop
2. **Protocol participation** — staked assets contribute to protocol TVL and signal long-term alignment

At mainnet, staking rewards may include a share of protocol swap fees in addition to points — subject to governance vote.

## Staking and the Discount NFT

The Discount Soulbound NFT gives fee discounts on swaps and limit orders. Staking gives points multiplier. They stack — you can hold a Tier 5 NFT and stake 10,000 CAREL simultaneously for maximum fee reduction and points earning.
